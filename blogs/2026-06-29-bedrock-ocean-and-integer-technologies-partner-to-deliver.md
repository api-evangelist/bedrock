Partnership pairs Bedrock's ocean intelligence with Integer's mission assurance software to boost operator confidence COLUMBIA, S.C., June 16th, 2026 — Integer Technologies (“Integer”), a leading provider of mission assurance and reliability software for maritime operations, today announced a partnership with Bedrock , a provider of ocean intelligence infrastructure. The companies can offer integrated undersea mission capabilities for the U.S. Navy, AUKUS, and special operations customers.
