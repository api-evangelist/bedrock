Oct 8th, 2025, Bedrock Ocean Exploration and PlanBlue announced a new strategic partnership that combines Bedrock’s advanced autonomous survey technology with PlanBlue’s AI-driven seafloor intelligence. By aligning their complementary capabilities, the two companies will deliver a new generation of decision-ready ocean data to accelerate infrastructure development, support maritime security, strengthen coastal resilience, and support environmental stewardship.
