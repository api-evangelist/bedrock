Bedrock scales to meet growing demand across defense, energy, and environmental monitoring in the U.S. and Europe Richmond, CA – April 7, 2026 – Bedrock Ocean Exploration , a leader in autonomous subsea data and intelligence, today announced the appointment of Matthew Tirman as Chief Executive Officer as the company builds on major deployments and rising demand across global offshore markets. Tirman brings more than two decades of experience in scaling deep-technology companies across geospatial intelligence, data platforms, and high-growth global commercial operations.
